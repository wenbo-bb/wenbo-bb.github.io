Responses

1. What aspect of your chosen visualisation first grabbed your attention? Explain the feature in terms of preattentive processing.

The purple line representing Africa at the bottom of the picture was the first to attract my attention. This is an example of preattentive processing, because purple can be immediately prominent in the contrast of other colors without deliberately looking for it.

2. Explain one Gestalt law that your chosen visualisation demonstrates.

This figure shows the law of similarity of Gestalt. Each continent is represented by a unique color, and all data points belonging to the same continent are connected by lines of the same color. This helps viewers to quickly identify which continent each line belongs to without reading each label.

3. Choose one of the quantitative variables visualised and discuss its visual accuracy.

The numerical variable "life expectancy" in the chart is displayed on the vertical axis. Visual accuracy is good, because the longitudinal axis starts from scratch, so the differences between continents are displayed proportionally.
